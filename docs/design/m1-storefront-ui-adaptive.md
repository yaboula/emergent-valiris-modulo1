Playful Minimalism and Brand-led UI within Modern Product-Centric Web Design —
Bold Graphic, Editorial & Soft Depth. The exact three selected Valiris references
and refinement guide govern the language: broad rounded heavy lettering, luminous
flexible colour, large culinary forms, deliberate crops and soft material depth.
Original ORILLA is intact. This is a proposed application, not final branding.

## Proposed decisions, exploratory values and open technical details

**Proposed visual decisions:** two intensities of experience; occasional culinary
characters only in expressive moments; quiet consultation fields; full wrapping
names and conditions; dark textual status on coral; recovery primary where relevant;
grouped visit facts; no sticky/fixed controls in this proposal.

**Exploratory values:** generated colour fields, organic profile surfaces, pill
geometry, material textures, type proportions and native image dimensions.
They are not approved tokens, breakpoints or technical font choices.

**Open technical details:** effective font/licence, editable composition medium,
semantic hierarchy, focus/keyboard/AT, live content maintenance, image alternatives,
200% text reflow, URL/origin compatibility, touch areas and responsive ranges.
44 × 44 px is a proposed comfort target for future controls, not a measured claim
on these raster images. Colour/geometry samples cannot certify executed touch.

Final persistent DESIGN.md consolidation remains after PC/phone contrast.
Phone and intermediate widths are deferred by the current founder instruction.
No proportional desktop shrink is accepted as later adaptation. Candidate changes:
stack offers with named formats intact, recompose schedule rows and navigation,
adjust crops, preserve story access, and reconsider PC if phone reveals a problem.
These are hypotheses, not completed mobile layouts or final breakpoints.
