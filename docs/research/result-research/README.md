# Module-specific discovery inputs

These seven reports were collected separately for M0.2 and are organised by product module. They are **research inputs**, not approved requirements or verified market facts. Several statements cite a publisher or vendor without a direct source URL; prices, percentages, legal interpretations, and feature claims require item-level verification before they appear in a product decision or customer-facing claim. Keep observations, hypotheses, and recommendations distinct when synthesising them.
