# Simulated scenario: repeat-guest cafe

**SIMULATED — FICTIONAL.** No interview took place. Every profile detail and response below is invented; no real person, business, transaction or metric is represented. Prepared 2026-09-28 using the [interview guide](script.md).

## Profile sheet (fictional assumptions)

- Independent cafe in Valencia; 18 seats and takeaway coffee through its existing POS.
- Predominantly walk-in local guests; the owner and one employee serve the counter.
- Paper stamp cards, Instagram and a printed menu; no table booking workflow.
- Constraint: a loyalty action cannot stall the queue or require an expensive POS integration.

The existing takeaway operation is context, not a proposal to add ordering to Valiris v1.

## Simulated conversation

**Question:** What happened the last time a regular forgot a loyalty card?

**Fictional response:** Staff stamped a second paper card. The guest later brought both cards and asked to combine them. Staff decided manually because there was no shared history.

**Question:** How did the last digital loyalty trial go?

**Fictional response:** Some guests saved a card, while others needed help finding it. The owner stopped explaining the process when the queue grew.

**Question:** What happened when you last contacted an absent regular?

**Fictional response:** The owner had not collected promotional permission and did not send anything. Recognising a person at the counter was easier than maintaining a marketing list.

**Question:** Describe the most recent need for table reservations.

**Fictional response:** There was none. For this cafe, a booking dashboard would be unused and would clutter the tasks staff actually perform.

**Question:** What would stop you changing the process?

**Fictional response:** Phone setup friction, customers without a suitable device, and having to explain where a reward went after changing phones. Paper cards remain an acceptable alternative.

## Scenario-derived hypotheses, not findings

- Stamp and reward history, customer recovery and understandable balances may matter more than campaign sophistication.
- Guest enrolment needs a practical fallback; Wallet adoption must be tested on Android and iOS.
- A confirmed eligible visit must remain distinct from a public QR scan.
- Hide inactive booking tasks through per-tenant module configuration; the seven-module portfolio does not imply every venue needs every module.

Public prompts: loyalty retrieval/workflow [RM-042–050](../review-ledger.md); [promotional permission](../legal.md). No public source confirms this cafe or these outcomes.

## What a real conversation must test

Walk through an actual reward dispute without customer identifiers. Observe an ordinary counter task and ask about current card costs, fraud and training. A program that guests do not adopt, or an existing POS loyalty feature that already works well, would weaken the companion-product hypothesis. This scenario provides no retention-uplift evidence.
