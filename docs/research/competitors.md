## Implications for Valiris

1. **The feature bundle is already available.** EterSystem directly challenges a menu + bookings + loyalty + reviews positioning; Qamarero advertises an integrated guest lifecycle too.
2. **A commission-free direct channel is not unique.** It also does not replace marketplace discovery. Valiris has no established consumer audience.
3. **Reliability and ease of use are candidate execution advantages.** [Public reviews](review-mining.md) support investigating them; they do not establish that Valiris outperforms current products.
4. **POS independence creates a trade-off.** It avoids migration into billing/ordering but requires a credible way to confirm actual visits. QR scans, reservations and campaign clicks are not purchases.
5. **Google native booking is not a free API checkbox.** Do not promise Actions Center distribution until eligibility and onboarding are resolved. A normal public booking URL is a different deliverable.

## Verification gaps

There has been no hands-on trial, vendor contact, quote request or contract review. Independent public review coverage of Qamarero and EterSystem was not established in this collection; curated vendor testimonials are excluded from the review count. Refresh prices at Gate 2 and before any commercial offer. Test plan-specific workflows in a later comparison if positioning depends on them.
