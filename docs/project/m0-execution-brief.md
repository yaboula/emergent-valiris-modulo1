# M0 execution brief

Version 0.1 · 1 October 2026 · Owner: Yahya, product owner and lead engineer.

**Status:** first executive contribution for review. Existing product direction is retained; priorities and working arrangements below are recommendations within that direction. This brief neither commits detailed launch capabilities nor closes M0.

**Owner clarification, 1 October 2026:** proceed by outcomes and dependencies without committed dates; examine costs when a concrete spending decision arises. A weekly-hours estimate or spending ceiling is not a prerequisite for starting this work.

## 1. Immediate objective and closure

Make Storefront + Digital Menu ready for experience design: a restaurant's public presence and understandable offering before and during a visit, with preparation, review, publication, maintenance, distribution, correction and continuity. Include necessary Core and a manageable onboarding/design/support service. QR is an entry route.

Use the [current roadmap](roadmap.md) and [functional definition](../research/storefront-digital-menu/functional-definition/functional-definition.es.md). General competitive exploration is closed. The seven-module timetable and former discovery gates are historical. Future modules remain unscheduled; ordering, payments, POS, delivery and invoicing remain outside this module.

**Recommendation:** start Product's capability inventory now; coordinate service and commercial constraints alongside it. Begin the affected M1 no-code design work when its brief is sufficiently resolved. Missing resource estimates must not block the inventory, but must prevent unsupported dates or sales promises.

M0 closes when the owner has reviewed the consolidated commitment and the following evidence exists:

- Every reference capability has explicit treatment: included, configurable within launch, later, connection-dependent or awaiting decision; with source, rationale, consolidating responsibility and dependency. Source labels are proposals, not automatic delivery phases.
- Diner, operator and buyer journeys have initial success criteria and representative cases. Accepted capabilities are not removed for technical convenience; material changes have an owner decision reference.
- Design, Engineering, Quality, Service and Commercial briefs identify inputs, outputs, review needs and dependencies. A designer can start without reconstructing the vision.
- Consequential open decisions have an owner, next action and affected work. An unresolved launch-defining choice blocks that commitment; a contained design question may continue under an explicit provisional assumption, without being sold as settled.
- Actual delivery/review capacity and missing tools, content or participant access are recorded. Unknown values stay unknown.

Documentary completion is not evidence of demand, usability, willingness to pay or margin. This contribution closes when Product has a usable assignment; full M0 closure also requires Product's consolidation.

## 6. Decisive risks and treatment

| Risk | Treatment / next responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| All recommendations become sales commitments, or accepted scope disappears in planning | Product records every capability and rationale; founder reviews material changes. Marketing uses only the status actually established. |
| Bespoke work and support consume unbounded effort | Preserve autonomous maintenance; Service proposes content volume, revision/change types, responsibilities, support window and escalation limits. Founder approves commercial commitments after evidence. |
| Import, publication or context changes produce plausible but incorrect public information | Product briefs include ambiguous prices, urgent sold-out edits alongside drafts, context conflicts and recovery; Engineering/Quality review those risks early. |
| Missing food data or stale translation appears confirmed | Keep declared/pending information and affected translations explicit; restaurant confirms its information. Seek focused specialist review when the chosen behaviour requires it. No compliance certification is implied. |
| External distribution, measurement or design makes excessive promises | Separate owned publication, generated printouts and connected destinations; distinguish digital actions from physical visits/sales. Retain accessibility and clear reading in both design routes. |
| Founder review or participant access becomes the bottleneck | Record capacity and pending access; limit simultaneous work, continue independent preparation, and block only the affected commitment/evidence. |
