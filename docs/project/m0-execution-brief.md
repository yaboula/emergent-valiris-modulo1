The completed M0 exit was audited against the following criteria (retained for traceability, not a new approval request):

- Every reference capability has explicit treatment: included, configurable within launch, later, connection-dependent or awaiting decision; with source, rationale, consolidating responsibility and dependency. Source labels are proposals, not automatic delivery phases.

Documentary completion is not evidence of demand, usability, willingness to pay or margin. Combined G1 delivered the commitment and briefs; M0 outcomes and Linear YAH-5 are closed. G2 produces the actual M1 evidence next.

## 6. Decisive risks and treatment

| Risk | Treatment / next responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| All recommendations become sales commitments, or accepted scope disappears in planning | Product records every capability and rationale; founder reviews material changes. Marketing uses only the status actually established. |
| Bespoke work and support consume unbounded effort | Preserve autonomous maintenance; Service proposes content volume, revision/change types, responsibilities, support window and escalation limits. Founder approves commercial commitments after evidence. |
| Import, publication or context changes produce plausible but incorrect public information | Product briefs include ambiguous prices, urgent sold-out edits alongside drafts, context conflicts and recovery; Engineering/Quality review those risks early. |
| Missing food data or stale translation appears confirmed | Keep declared/pending information and affected translations explicit; restaurant confirms its information. Seek focused specialist review when the chosen behaviour requires it. No compliance certification is implied. |
| External distribution, measurement or design makes excessive promises | Separate owned publication, generated printouts and connected destinations; distinguish digital actions from physical visits/sales. Retain accessibility and clear reading in both design routes. |
| Founder review or participant access becomes the bottleneck | Record capacity and pending access; limit simultaneous work, continue independent preparation, and block only the affected commitment/evidence. |
