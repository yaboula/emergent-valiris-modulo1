## Finding G4-01 — P2: draft-save uncertainty and competing draft edits have no reviewable recovery branch

- **Classification:** missing visible behavior contract, not an observed runtime data-loss defect. It affects the first-path preparation boundary, not a new capability request.
- **Path / states:** `docs/design/m1-connected-journey.md`, O02 -> O03/O05; save/leave -> unknown result or changed draft.
- **Verified evidence:** lines 222-228 describe an unpublished editable proposal, save with saved/unsaved result, save/discard on leaving and content retention after failed save. Lines 232-236 then review a saved proposal. X02 lines 310-312 reconciles editorial *publication*, urgent availability and undo; it does not identify a draft-save outcome. X03 line 318 compares a newly changed *current publication* against the proposal. O05 line 242 rechecks the reviewed source, but the document does not establish which confirmed draft revision O03 reviews after an uncertain or competing save.
- **Applicable expectations:** `docs/product/m0-journeys-and-criteria.md` R04/R05, lines 26-27, and J4.4 line 82 require explicit conflict/review and retained unrelated draft work. The operator brief also requires honest unsaved/failed-operation and changed-underfoot recovery.
- **Reproduction using C1:** start with published coffee at 1,90 EUR and its private proposed price at 2,00 EUR; keep the private croissant-description draft unrelated. Save the coffee proposal, but lose its acknowledgement. In a separate competing branch, a second authorised editor saves 2,10 EUR to the same coffee draft while the first editor still sees 2,00 EUR. The first editor chooses Revisar cambios, or leaves after choosing Save. Follow the written destinations without assuming server behavior.
- **Expected:** distinguish locally entered 2,00 EUR, last confirmed saved draft revision and uncertain/current saved outcome. A competing saved revision requires current-versus-proposed draft review with preserved authorised work, before a selected publication can claim to publish a reviewed revision. The coffee publication and croissant draft remain unchanged until an authorised known publication result. An unknown save must not silently mean confirmed saved, confirmed failed or safely disposable.
- **Actually described:** known save/failure and later publication conflict are covered, but there is no concrete save-unknown/status/retry branch or competing-draft-save resolution. A reader cannot determine what is reviewed, whether leaving discards the local proposal, or how to reconcile without guessing. This is an absence of contract; the document does not explicitly instruct an overwrite.
- **Proposed correction:** extend O02 or add a linked save-recovery variant with copy such as "No hemos podido confirmar el guardado de este borrador"; identify the proposal, venue/item and saved revision/result when known. Preserve permitted local entered work, offer a status/reopen check, and distinguish confirmed save, confirmed no-effect, changed draft and remaining uncertainty. A changed draft shows current/proposed values and a deliberate retain/review choice. Only a confirmed coherent reviewed proposal may proceed to its publication selection. If review uses a deliberately retained local proposal, label that separately and revalidate the actual saved/current source before authorisation. Do not make a denied venue readable merely to retain the form.
- **Correction check:** re-read (1) save committed/acknowledgement lost, (2) save did not occur, (3) competing 2,10 EUR draft save, and (4) edit/venue access revoked during recovery. Each must identify allowed content, last confirmed source, unsaved/unknown status and exact next destination, preserve unrelated croissant work, and produce no public price change or automatic overwrite. Technical concurrency/idempotency/persistence guarantees still belong to #4 and later implementation verification.
- **Responsibility/checkpoint:** G2 supplies visible states; G1 consolidates operational policy; G3 specifies technical mechanisms in M2. Required before claiming the affected preparation/recovery branch resolved. No founder scope decision is needed for this routine correction.
