## G4-J15-01 product consequence and correction recommendation

Evidence: J15-A06 lines 307-311 says deleted/renamed categories are flagged for review. J15-P02 line 316 supplies a diet-only candidate rule when no categories are selected; line 322 defines multiple-category matching and forbids silent diet relaxation. Q17 line 496 names a renamed-category contrast but does not supply the missing lifecycle result. ADR-0010 line 41 lists category lifecycle as a reviewable proposal with technical contracts pending. CP menu/correction rules do not resolve the private preference transition.

Counterexample: authorised A explicitly saves Vinos plus Vegetarian at Mesa del Patio. The last selected category is removed or replaced while a recommendation request is pending. A's preference cannot silently be interpreted as deliberately choosing no tastes, causing all confirmed Vegetarian candidates to appear under the old personalised explanation. The document currently says to flag the change but leaves the next visible state, treatment of saved reference and permitted result undefined. It does not affirmatively instruct that incorrect expansion.

Recommend retaining the unavailable reference visibly and routing to preference review or an honest affected no-match/fallback state. A deliberate new selection, reset or reconfirmation may change the saved inputs; operator category removal cannot silently make that choice for the diner. For multiple tastes, any remaining valid matching must use actually retained selected categories and disclose the invalid selection. If the last taste is unavailable, do not apply the voluntary no-tastes rule until an explicit reviewed choice. Public menu/venue suggestions stay usable and separately labelled.

Distinguish a cosmetic rename of the same confirmed category identity from retirement, substitution or uncertain identity/meaning. A new similarly named category is not automatically equivalent. G3 specifies identity/lifecycle/revision enforcement in M2; G2 must supply the visible state and action/result now. This preserves scope and explicit-input intent without requiring a new founder question.

Trade-off: silent removal shortens recovery but changes the candidate meaning without diner intent. Explicit recovery adds a focused review step and may temporarily suppress affected personalised results; it retains honest control and public fallback. No efficiency, safety or recommendation benefit is established.
