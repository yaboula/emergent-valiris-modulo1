Record source/composition/version, start state, first action, interpreted answer/context, errors, assistance, recovery and missing inputs. Count steps only as an observation; do not convert three clicks into acceptance. Distinguish misunderstood labels/structure from absent content and interaction failures. Do not test illustrative unverified food facts as a safety assurance. Proposed serious failures include wrong price context, hidden essential conditions, a false food conclusion or a lost critical return; apply the existing criteria with G1/G4 rather than invent a success score.

Before adding a card sort or tree test, identify a remaining classification/label question that actual ORILLA content cannot resolve through these situated tasks. No participant method substitutes for G2.03 interaction review, UI/accessibility application checks or independent G4 review. Document absent inputs, how to obtain them, the affected blocks and how the result would revise the proposal.
