## 2. Findings and acceptance limit

One author correction was made while writing: the destination diagram initially only showed detail returning to the menu even though the table preserved a Home-preview origin. The diagram now explicitly includes D01 → H02. This is an authored consistency correction, not a G4 finding or a successful navigation test.

IA-C01–IA-C22 are preserved from the first published proposal. The v0.2 method refinement adds IA-C23–IA-C26; no earlier contrast is reclassified as participant validation. The proposed [participant protocol and method adaptation](rampstack-adaptation.md) identify observations to obtain later, with zero sessions conducted.

No material contradiction was reproduced in these bounded written contrasts. That verdict is limited to the architecture's stated rules and the frozen source examples. Actual ORILLA narrative, dishes, prices, variants, visit conditions and food-example states remain absent; the corresponding walkthroughs cannot be claimed completed with representative ORILLA content. The requests name those blocked contributions, and UI can inspect the preliminary structure independently.

Before consolidating the application, obtain actual content/copy and SEO/interaction/UI returns, inspect PC and deliberate phone compositions with their text/context/states, then seek independent G4 review and authorized participant/device evidence at the existing checkpoints. Price/context mismatch, draft leak, hidden essential condition, false food assurance, misleading factual language or lost critical return stops the affected acceptance until corrected. Document integrity checks are recorded separately in `checks.json`; they do not resolve those proof obligations.
