### Reading rhythm and alternatives

Recommended rhythm: a strong recognisable opening → culinary method and exact
choices → human/table meaning → practical reassurance. One repeated card layout
would flatten these different tasks. Retain a coherent type/material/colour grammar
while changing density and composition according to the visitor's question.

The existing partial hero is a reference for identity/food scale, not a frozen
layout into which the rest must be fitted. A full-page composition may require
adjusting it. Functional reading fields remain calm; essential text stays complete.

Character presence is a **situated preference explicitly selected by the founder**:
occasional culinary characters integrated in the composition. Character-free
product/object expression also exists in the selected reference set.
This is not a request to reopen the chosen branding or impose a permanent cast.

## Evidence required beyond the main composition

The principal PC proposal must show all four blocks together or as clearly
connected contiguous PC compositions, with no unexplained omissions. ImageGen's
actual output size/text fidelity will determine whether additional native-size
section images are necessary; a tiny whole-page thumbnail is not reading evidence.

Separate bounded PC samples should demonstrate V02 with its compatible prices and
milk uncertainty, flan lifecycle/withdrawal/unknown state, dated kitchen exception
versus pending visit data, missing resources/story, actual Spanish fallback and
unconfirmed refresh. Menu/detail are **bridge samples**, not a full Digital Menu
rebuild. Direct entry and actual-origin returns remain annotated; drawn actions
do not demonstrate navigation.

Six R10 checks: recognition/fiction/task; full current offer/format/unit/minimum;
story/visit/date; food and unknown states; language/origin/recovery; essential
reading independent of resource/motion. Each needs source + actual image location
and a result or explicit pending checkpoint. No tool score substitutes for these.
