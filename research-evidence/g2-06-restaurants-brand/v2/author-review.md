## What was inspected

## Findings and corrections

1. The first sensory scene had ambiguous duplicated facial marks and leaf-like anatomy. A targeted refinement simplified the faces and upward silhouette before extending that exploration.
2. B's first image was too painterly/nostalgic. Its refinement retained the social action and removed grain, extra background people and unnecessary detail for a more contemporary flat language.
3. The founder proposed fruit, vegetables, kitchen objects and plates while viewing work in progress. A was developed into plate/pear/aubergine/cup; the earlier sensory scene, study, render and keyframes remain superseded process evidence.
4. Sheet 04 initially placed the four object labels below the acting row. Moving them before the silhouette row corrected the label/image correspondence.
5. Ink on red measured 4.43:1, below the conservative 4.5:1 threshold used here. The red swatch became a graphic band with labels on cream; guide/pairing rule were corrected. Final native text minimum is 4.72:1, with no immediate-parent overflow reported.

## Direction judgement

A has four recognisable object contours and meaningful group acting; plate rim, pear waist, aubergine curve and cup handle distinguish members while faces/hands/limbs link them. The welcoming scene has foreground, architecture, table and relationships. The 3D scene translates the same family into matte material and light. B creates warmth through people-like social gestures; it changes semantic construction and scene dependence rather than just colour. A remains recommended after the founder's kitchen-object steering.

Comparison with references: A does not use Duo/owl anatomy, Marshmallow stacks/double arches, Headspace's orange sun, Gorilla animal or Eat Curious's combined palette/type/boundary recipe. Shared broad techniques such as articulated limbs, cream hands and scene staging are not evidence of exclusive design. This is visual author comparison, not a clearance search or a recognition study.

## Production limits still visible

The silhouette study retains a slight plate-edge detail; a pure solid mask and compact-size optical drawings belong to production. Hands, stem/calyx, facial placement and limb attachment need final normalisation. The 3D plate has a slight surface/face-depth interpretation that requires a model decision; this is not a locked production model. Botanical green and raster shading are not approved tokens. A's SVG terminal cut-outs use cream; genuine knockout variants remain pending. Tiny favicons are not demonstrated by shrinking the full named lockup. B's scene is a concept, not a finished social-character repertoire.

These limits do not prevent comparison of the two directions. They prevent treating the conceptual resources as a final asset library. The plate storyboard communicates anticipation, welcome and rest; timings, curves, reduced motion and implementation testing are absent. ES/DE copy has no specialist localisation approval. G6/G2.04/SEO/GEO/G2.05 and independent G4 review remain checkpoints, not received consultations.
