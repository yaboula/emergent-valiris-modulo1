# Reference analysis — the mechanics of a living identity

G2.06 author analysis, 3 October 2026. This is the focused rework requested by the founder, not a reopened competitive benchmark. Five supplied references inform two new creative territories. No conversion, customer preference, exclusivity or accessibility outcome is inferred from appearance.

## Method and source boundary

The supplied Eat Curious screenshot PDF contains three pages and Duolingo four. Both are raster captures with no extractable text. All seven pages were rendered and inspected; copies are in [reference-evidence](reference-evidence/). The first WebM shows Ragged Edge's **Marshmallow** case study; the second shows **Headspace**, not Duolingo. They contain VP9 1920 × 1080 video and no audio stream. Their last decoded frame timestamps are approximately 41.290 and 25.869 seconds (1,217 and 777 decoded frames).

Sequential samples at 2 fps cover both full timelines: 83 and 52 images, arranged into seven and five contact sheets. Four sequences were additionally sampled at 8 fps: Marshmallow logo 4.7–6.3 s (13 frames), Marshmallow type 12.1–14.8 s (22), Headspace chat 7.0–9.0 s (16), Headspace ending 19.7–23.3 s (29). The 215 reviewed samples overlap; this is not a claim to have manually inspected every original frame. Contact-sheet labels are approximate nominal times, not verified animation triggers. Native browser scroll and page animation are distinguished where visibly possible; code, exact easing, input response and reduced-motion behaviour are unknown.

The Gorilla sheet was supplied as an image. Its author, official brand status and reuse licence are unidentified. The screenshots/frames remain reference evidence, never production artwork. Local original hashes and derivative hashes are in the manifest. Primary author documents below clarify intention; they do not replace observation of the supplied captures. These documents were consulted during the planning analysis on 3 October; no new general research was required for execution.

## 1. Eat Curious — colour creates a place, food creates relevance

**Observed, page 1.** A deep green field gives the opening one dominant atmosphere. Heavy cream condensed lettering acts at architectural scale; the stacked mark and small hand-drawn pink/yellow interventions occupy different scales. Cropped food bowls enter the lower boundary. They have a compositional purpose: connect abstract brand energy to something edible. The following acid-yellow product field changes tempo, with large food images and alternating coloured panels. Partly visible edge cards suggest more content, but the capture does not demonstrate carousel controls or their usability. A quieter cream feature row introduces breathing room.

**Observed, page 2.** Recipe photography, green fields, pink card bases, distributor marks, testimonials and a large narrative illustration alternate roles. The illustration makes a generous metaphor; it does not have to be a literal ingredient mascot. Photography says what the product is, while illustration says how the brand sees the world. Any dark treatment on recipe photos is an observed capture state; its trigger is unknown.

**Observed, page 3.** A pink subscription zone gives the ending a strong new colour beat. Large surfaces, small details and readable groupings create rhythm. The cookie overlay is a separate compliance layer, not an identity construction rule.

**Author-declared.** [Fiasco's case study](https://fiasco.design/work/plant-based-brand-eat-curious/) explains the identity's combination of bold type, food imagery and narrative illustration. It names Obviously as the display family and Miguel Angel Camprubi as the illustrator. Those are provenance observations, not resources licensed for Valiris. Motion mentioned by the author is not something the PDF itself proves.

**Inference for Valiris.** Increase the amount and scale of intentional colour; make gastronomic character visible through material, gesture and situation. Alternate high expression with quiet reading. A few colour chips beside a neutral layout cannot produce this effect. The line must also survive scarce restaurant photography: an authored scene can introduce atmosphere while actual menu information remains text.

**Do not transfer.** The combined green/pink/yellow palette, condensed lettering and wavy boundary treatment would be too recognisable as the source. Product-specific nutrition, taste, storage or allergen promises cannot become claims about restaurants. Generated brand illustration cannot stand in for photographs of real offered dishes.

## 2. Duolingo — construction rules make acting possible

**Observed, page 1.** The white opening is lively because the ensemble is doing something. Poses, gaze, bodily exaggeration and overlaps create a group rather than a sticker lineup. Subsequent modules connect characters and objects to a specific message: learning, practice, science or personalisation. Large simple shapes support readable acting; the faces are not the only source of personality.

**Observed, pages 2–4.** Alternating illustration/text gives a stable reading rhythm. A large blue atmosphere and floating devices create a different presentation intensity. Super Duolingo's dark campaign treatment is a sub-brand expression, not evidence that Valiris needs dark mode. Other services adapt the scene while retaining family resemblance. The bright footer gives the long page a recognisable ending; practical language and cookie information remain understandable.

**Author-declared.** [Building character](https://blog.duolingo.com/building-character/) explains how recurring construction traits from Duo informed the cast. [Shape language](https://blog.duolingo.com/shape-language-duolingos-art-style/) describes simple forms used for expression and storytelling. The [logo guidance](https://design.duolingo.com/identity/logos) relates a letter flourish to the mascot's brows; the [typography guidance](https://design.duolingo.com/identity/typography) distinguishes branded headlines from reading. These source-specific fonts and anatomy are not Valiris assets.

**Inference for Valiris.** Define stable silhouettes, hand proportions and facial placement before proliferating poses. A character should receive, lean, notice or make space. Its geometry should influence lettering and framing so the identity can persist when the figure disappears. White space works when what remains has authored presence.

**Do not transfer.** Owl anatomy, large white eyes, detached feet, learning rewards, coins or streaks would import both visual identity and an unrelated product metaphor. No animation was observed in the PDF. Full language support, learning results and conversion are outside this evidence.

## 3. Marshmallow — elastic forms connect type, characters and objects

**Observed, 0–4 s.** Introductory agency typography and navigation frame the case. They must not be confused with Marshmallow's own identity.

**Observed, 4.7–6.3 s.** The pink mark and lettering deform, incline and recover. The movement has anticipation and resolution rather than permanent floating. Around 6.5–7.5 s, a face is enlarged into a graphic plane: character can operate at the scale of a whole composition.

**Observed, 9.5–14.8 s.** Digital applications appear in a staged interior. The following type/face sequence shows elastic settling and expression changes. Because scrolling is recorded simultaneously, the capture cannot determine which motion is timed, viewport-triggered or tied to scroll position.

**Observed, 16–28.5 s.** Small objects, physical-looking character sculptures and interface fragments demonstrate the same vocabulary at different scales. Around 19.5–21 s, many silhouette assemblies form a family; around 21–23 s, volume and materials give that grammar spatial presence. Mobile factual content remains organised even when expressive characters surround it. Variety is not simply a recolour of one pose.

**Observed, 29.5–41 s.** Agency claims, testimony and closing contact sections belong to the case-study presentation. Its later teal/white ending is not automatically a Marshmallow palette rule. Reported business statistics do not establish causation by identity.

**Author-declared.** [Ragged Edge's case](https://raggededge.com/partnerships/marshmallow) connects a welcoming modular character language to a strategy of celebrating people previously overlooked by insurance. That strategic reason explains the family; Valiris needs its own reason.

**Inference for Valiris.** Culinary sensations offer an intelligible basis for different body temperaments. Curves, cuts and counterspaces can connect those bodies to letters, large fields and a sculptural table. One coherent 3D scene tests this translation without committing to full 3D production. Motion should express the material's behaviour and then let the viewer read.

**Do not transfer.** Stacked pink marsh forms, double-arch silhouettes, big white eye pairs or the same interior staging would be imitation. The recording provides neither animation implementation nor commercial proof for our product.

## 4. Headspace — small expressions coexist with a complete environment

**Observed, 0–6 s.** A practical service structure combines neutral surfaces, photographic credibility and bounded bright fields. The persistent header and named pathways keep orientation separate from decorative expression. Small faces accompany understandable content rather than replacing labels.

**Observed, 7–9 s.** Prepared chat bubbles introduce an anticipation state around a small expressive form. This is a presentation sequence; no live exchange or outcome was tested. It does not justify adding AI/chat functionality to Valiris.

**Observed, 9.5–19 s.** Testimony, partner material, coloured separators, content covers and a quieter FAQ use different intensities. Illustration, photography and factual reading have different jobs. The yellow expression strip creates a beat in the page; it is not a demand to animate every element.

**Observed, 19.7–23.3 s.** A yellow landscape, orange face, clouds and progressively appearing coloured arc form a complete scene. Relationships between foreground and background produce atmosphere. The final call to action has a place inside this world; the ordinary footer can follow without carrying the same expressive load.

**Author-declared.** Headspace's primary [illustration](https://live.standards.site/headspace/illustration), [motion](https://live.standards.site/headspace/motion), [typography](https://live.standards.site/headspace/typography) and [photography](https://live.standards.site/headspace/photography) guidance describes coherent construction, purposeful pacing and a relationship between image and message. Its prescribed palette limits, faces and timing belong to Headspace, not to our specification.

**Inference for Valiris.** Design environments and relationships, not only mascots. Let expressive objects occupy an opening or ending, and let consultation remain readable. Hospitality should feel warm and energetic here rather than importing a wellness temperament wholesale.

**Do not transfer.** Orange smiling suns, signature expressions, rainbow scenery or clinical/wellbeing claims. The recording cannot establish focus, keyboard operation, screen-reader semantics or reduced-motion behaviour.

## 5. Gorilla — anatomy remains recognisable while the task changes

**Observed.** Large arms, substantial hands, a compact lower body, a white facial counterspace and a recurring head profile remain recognisable across shopping, running, hesitation, celebration and cycling. Props explain a specific action. The upper composition links a character's physical gesture with a large letterform. This is a stronger relationship than placing unrelated type beside an isolated mascot.

**Inference for Valiris.** Build a family gene with repeatable hands, limbs, face placement and contour tension. Variation should change attitude and action without accidentally changing species or construction. Category relevance belongs in situations of hospitality; delivery bikes, payment gestures and orders would imply functions absent from this module.

**Unknown.** This is an attached concept sheet, not a verified live brand site. Authorship, original commission, source URL and licensing are unknown. No performance or research result can be derived from it.

## Comparative conclusion and application

The references solve different problems: Eat Curious anchors energy in food; Duolingo builds meaningful acting; Marshmallow connects modular anatomy with every medium; Headspace creates environments and intensity changes; Gorilla preserves construction through poses. Their shared discipline is **few specific rules, rich application**.

The rejected Valiris v1 changed palette and font while repeating a document-like grid. Its primitive figures had no acting vocabulary, setting or meaningful typographic relationship. Passing opaque text contrast did not answer that artistic weakness. v2 must be judged through the two full scenes, family study, original lettering, two reading intensities and spatial translation. The comparison is an author review against five supplied examples, not legal clearance or proof of unique public recognition.
