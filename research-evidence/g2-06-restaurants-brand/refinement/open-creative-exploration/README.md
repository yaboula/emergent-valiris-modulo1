# Open creative exploration after direction selection

3 October 2026 · G2.06 · Three generated concepts, not final branding.

The founder requested more creative examples after selecting the character-free culinary triptych. Selection provides a working direction; it does not prevent exploration or require discarding the four retained character scenes. These studies vary their visual idea, camera and relationship to the product, while retaining broad geometric lettering, vivid colour, culinary forms and soft depth. They are line-brand marketing studies, not real restaurant pages or an implemented website.

| Study | Creative mechanism | Useful contribution | Limit and next question |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [01 — Reveal stage](01-abre-algo-diferente.png) | An oversized serving dome reveals a folded menu on a shallow platform; a curved fork-like rail leads toward it | Culinary hospitality turns the product presentation into an event, with the product clearly visible | More sculptural than the character scenes. Reveal/fold is a static metaphor, not commissioned behaviour; test whether the dome and rail help or compete with the product |
| [02 — Culinary lettering](02-sabor-tipografico.png) | Folded material, utensil handles, spoon bowls and a plate construct the word SABOR in an overhead still life | Strongest author recommendation for a new graphic signature: kitchen anatomy, lettering and colour become one composition without characters | Display lettering must be drawn for each word; this is not a font, alphabet or logo. Recognition is an author visual judgement, not public research. Product presentation is secondary |
| [03 — Café collaboration](03-cafeteria-con-caracter.png) | A cup and moka pot jointly present an ivory ticket-like product ribbon across a café counter | Connects adaptable object characters, setting and product; adds a café situation without assigning fixed roles | The ribbon is a promotional staging metaphor, not an interface or configuration control. “Storefront”, “Carta digital” and “Tu contenido” are conceptual labels, not approval of a new offer or package |

All three are complementary possibilities within the direction. No new founder selection is claimed. The third scene does not reserve café sections for these characters; any appropriate culinary object can be chosen or created locally. Colours remain compositional choices, not a closed palette. Concept headlines are author proposals pending content/commercial review; no conversion or commercial advantage is claimed.

## Production and evidence boundary

Built-in `image_gen.imagegen` produced the raster studies using existing versioned imagery as visual references. The [manifest](manifest.json) preserves full prompts, input roles, source filenames, rights/status, dimensions and hashes. No exact font binary was used, no editable resources or physical tableware were made, and the illustrated objects/food are fictional/generated. No exclusivity or third-party rights clearance is claimed.

Author inspection checks visible wording, framing, product hierarchy, character action and whether the visual mechanisms actually differ. [Checks](checks.json) records PNG integrity, source/copy equality, local links and preserved ORILLA. These checks do not establish functional UI, motion, contrast ratios, localisation, accessibility, user validation or independent G4 review. No new software tests apply to these visual/documentary studies.

Native lettering and coherence stay with G2.06; G2.08 owns resource production; G2.07/G2.03 actual composition/behaviour. Specialists are not activated here. The selected triptych, previous scenes, original ORILLA, original v2 and shared G1 sources remain intact. This exploration does not close #15 or M1.
